COMPUTER

what is computer ?

A computer is an electronic machine that stores, retrieves, and processes data to turn it into useful information.

Types of computer:

Computers are generally classified into 5 main types based on size and purpose, and into 3 types based on how they process data.

By Size and Power

  1. Supercomputers: The fastest and most powerful systems used for complex tasks like weather forecasting and space research.
  2. Mainframe Computers: Large, secure systems used by banks and governments to process millions of transactions at once.
  3. Minicomputers (Midrange): Medium-sized multi-processing systems used by small businesses or research labs.
  4. Microcomputers (Personal Computers): Affordable devices for individual use, including desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones.
  5. Embedded Computers: Tiny systems built into everyday appliances like washing machines, smartwatches, and cars to handle specific tasks.

What is processers?
